# Hemanth SR · Watch

A streaming-style way to browse the YouTube channel [@hemanth.sr5](https://www.youtube.com/@hemanth.sr5), plus outlier videos from other creators in AI and business.

- Static files only: no server, no database, no logins, no forms, no tracking.
- Every video plays through YouTube's official privacy-enhanced embedded player and belongs to its creator. Nothing is downloaded or re-hosted.
- Outlier figures = a video's views divided by its channel's median views, from a scan on 4 Nov 2026.
- A strict Content Security Policy allows only this site's own files, YouTube thumbnails and the YouTube player.
